# Copywriting

## Principle
Match a real customer problem to a credible promise and a low-friction next action.

## Workflow
1. Define audience, awareness level, offer, proof, objection, and desired action.
2. Lead with the most relevant outcome or pain, not brand adjectives.
3. Translate features into concrete consequences and use proof where available.
4. Handle the main objection without hype or unverifiable claims.
5. End with one primary call to action and remove competing distractions.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Claims are specific and supportable.
- Copy is understandable without internal jargon.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
