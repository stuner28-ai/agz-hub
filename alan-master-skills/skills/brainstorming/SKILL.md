# Brainstorming

## Principle
Create diverse options first, then converge using explicit criteria.

## Workflow
1. Restate the objective and constraints.
2. Generate options across different mechanisms, not cosmetic variations.
3. Include at least one conservative, one ambitious, and one unconventional direction when appropriate.
4. Cluster duplicates and eliminate dominated options.
5. Rank a shortlist against the user's actual objective and explain the tradeoffs.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Diversity precedes ranking.
- Ideas remain feasible enough to evaluate.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
