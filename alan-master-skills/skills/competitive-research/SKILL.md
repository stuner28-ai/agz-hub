# Competitive Research

## Principle
Convert competitor information into actionable positioning and strategic choices.

## Workflow
1. Define the customer job and comparison dimensions first.
2. Gather current first-party product, pricing, and positioning data.
3. Separate table-stakes, differentiators, and claimed features that are not independently validated.
4. Map competitor strengths, weaknesses, target segments, and switching barriers.
5. Identify gaps worth pursuing and gaps that are unattractive to copy.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Comparison uses the same dimensions across competitors.
- Recommendations are based on customer value, not feature count.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
