# Deep Research

## Principle
Build an evidence-backed answer from independent, high-quality sources.

## Workflow
1. Define the research question, scope, recency, and decision relevance.
2. Search broadly, then prioritize primary sources, official data, and strong secondary analysis.
3. Track claims to sources and note disagreement or uncertainty.
4. Actively search for disconfirming evidence and missing perspectives.
5. Synthesize findings into conclusions, confidence, limitations, and next actions.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Material factual claims are traceable to evidence.
- Freshness matches the user's requested time window.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
