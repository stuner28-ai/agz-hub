# Customer Research

## Principle
Extract patterns from evidence while preserving individual context and avoiding leading conclusions.

## Workflow
1. Define the research question and participant criteria.
2. Prefer questions about past behavior, concrete incidents, alternatives, and tradeoffs.
3. Capture exact pain, trigger, workaround, consequence, and decision process.
4. Code recurring themes without flattening meaningful differences.
5. Separate findings, confidence, and product implications.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Stated preference is not treated as observed behavior.
- Quotes are not generalized beyond evidence.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
