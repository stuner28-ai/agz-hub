# Summarization

## Principle
Summaries are selective representations, not shorter copies.

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, purpose, required level of detail, and non-negotiable facts.
2. Extract decisions, claims, evidence, actions, dates, and unresolved issues as relevant.
3. Group information by meaning rather than source order.
4. Preserve caveats and disagreement that could change interpretation.
5. Remove repetition and low-value detail without inventing connections.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- No material decision or qualification is lost for the requested purpose.
- Summary distinguishes decided, proposed, and unresolved items.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
