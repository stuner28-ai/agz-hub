# Trend Analysis

## Principle
Distinguish durable trend from noise using multiple signals over time.

## Workflow
1. Define the trend hypothesis and relevant time horizon.
2. Collect independent signals such as adoption, search, investment, regulation, supply, usage, and revenue.
3. Separate leading indicators from lagging indicators.
4. Check seasonality, base effects, one-off events, and survivorship bias.
5. Classify trend strength, durability, uncertainty, and implications.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- At least two independent signal types support strong conclusions.
- Short spikes are not extrapolated blindly.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
