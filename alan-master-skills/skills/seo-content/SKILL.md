# SEO Content

## Principle
Satisfy the searcher's task better than competing pages before optimizing keywords.

## Workflow
1. Identify primary intent, subquestions, entity/topic coverage, and likely SERP format.
2. Build a useful structure that answers the core question early and covers necessary depth.
3. Use descriptive titles, headings, internal links, and structured data when appropriate.
4. Add first-hand evidence, original analysis, examples, or tools that create unique value.
5. Review for accuracy, crawlability, duplication, and conversion path.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Content is useful without keyword stuffing.
- Search optimization does not degrade readability or factual quality.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
