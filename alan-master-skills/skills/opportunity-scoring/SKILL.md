# Opportunity Scoring

## Principle
Rank opportunities by expected value, uncertainty, capital, speed, and strategic fit.

## Workflow
1. Define comparable opportunity units and time horizon.
2. Estimate upside, probability, time-to-validation, capital, operating burden, downside, and scalability.
3. Normalize assumptions and make uncertainty visible.
4. Calculate or qualitatively compare risk-adjusted return; test sensitivity to key assumptions.
5. Recommend a portfolio order: validate now, queue, monitor, or reject.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Scores do not hide weak assumptions.
- Fast validation receives value when uncertainty is high.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
