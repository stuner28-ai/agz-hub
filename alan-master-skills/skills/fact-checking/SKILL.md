# Fact Checking

## Principle
Determine what is true, false, misleading, unverified, or context-dependent.

## Workflow
1. Break compound claims into atomic checkable statements.
2. Locate the strongest available primary or authoritative evidence.
3. Check dates, definitions, denominators, and original context.
4. Compare independent sources and identify what would falsify the claim.
5. Return a verdict per claim with confidence and concise evidence.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Verdict scope matches the evidence.
- Absence of evidence is not presented as evidence of absence.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
