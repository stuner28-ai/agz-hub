# Sales Strategy

## Principle
Align sales effort with customer pain, buying process, value, and deal economics.

## Workflow
1. Define ICP, trigger events, buyer roles, and disqualifiers.
2. Map discovery questions to business impact and current alternatives.
3. Build a value case tied to measurable outcomes.
4. Design next steps around the customer's buying process, not seller activity alone.
5. Track pipeline by evidence-based stage exit criteria.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Pipeline stages require buyer evidence.
- Claims and commitments remain accurate and supportable.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
