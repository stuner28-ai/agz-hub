# Growth Marketing

## Principle
Build growth from measurable loops and channel economics rather than isolated campaigns.

## Workflow
1. Define target segment, promise, funnel, and north-star outcome.
2. Map acquisition channels to intent, cost, reach, and creative requirements.
3. Identify activation moments and retention loops.
4. Set channel-level experiments with budgets, guardrails, and success thresholds.
5. Scale only repeatable winners while monitoring incrementality and saturation.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Growth metrics connect to business outcomes.
- Paid growth includes CAC and payback constraints.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
