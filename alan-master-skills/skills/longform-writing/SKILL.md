# Longform Writing

## Principle
Design the reader's progression before drafting prose.

## Workflow
1. Define audience, promise, desired transformation, evidence needs, and tone.
2. Build an outline where each section has a distinct job.
3. Draft for clarity and specificity using examples, transitions, and varied rhythm.
4. Support factual claims appropriately and remove unsupported filler.
5. Revise for structure first, then sentence quality, then polish.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Every section advances the promise.
- Opening, body, and conclusion serve different functions.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
