# Experiment Design

## Principle
Design experiments that can actually change a decision.

## Workflow
1. Write hypothesis, treatment, primary metric, guardrails, and decision rule before launch.
2. Define population, randomization or comparison method, duration, and minimum detectable effect when relevant.
3. Prevent contamination, peeking, metric switching, and multiple-comparison abuse.
4. Check instrumentation before exposing users.
5. Analyze effect size, uncertainty, heterogeneity, and practical significance.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Success criteria are precommitted.
- Statistical significance is not confused with business significance.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
