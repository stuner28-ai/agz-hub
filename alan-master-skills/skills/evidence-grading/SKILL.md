# Evidence Grading

## Principle
Grade evidence by directness, reliability, methodology, recency, and independence.

## Workflow
1. List the material claims that drive the conclusion.
2. For each claim, identify source type, directness, date, sample/method, and incentives.
3. Downgrade circular citations, anecdotes, marketing claims, and unreplicated findings.
4. Separate confidence in individual facts from confidence in the overall conclusion.
5. State what new evidence would most change confidence.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Confidence language matches evidence quality.
- Quantity of sources does not substitute for source quality.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
