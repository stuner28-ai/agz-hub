# Dashboard Design

## Principle
A dashboard should answer recurring decisions at a glance, not display every available metric.

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, decisions, cadence, and escalation thresholds.
2. Choose a small hierarchy of outcome, driver, and diagnostic metrics.
3. Use comparisons against target, prior period, and relevant segments.
4. Design charts around questions and anomalies; include definitions and freshness.
5. Add drill-down paths for diagnosis without crowding the top view.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Every visual supports a decision or diagnostic question.
- Status and trend are distinguishable.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
