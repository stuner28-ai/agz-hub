# Unit Economics

## Principle
Model economics at the unit that actually scales.

## Workflow
1. Define the economic unit and period.
2. Separate revenue, direct variable costs, payment fees, fulfillment, support, acquisition, and refunds.
3. Calculate contribution margin and acquisition payback before using long-horizon LTV.
4. Model retention/cohort behavior and uncertainty where LTV matters.
5. Run sensitivity on the variables most likely to change the conclusion.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Fixed and variable costs are not mixed silently.
- LTV assumptions are explicit and cohort-aware.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
