# Systems Thinking

## Principle
Optimize the whole system rather than a single visible symptom.

## Workflow
1. Map actors, components, flows, constraints, and feedback loops.
2. Identify bottlenecks, coupling, incentives, and delayed effects.
3. Distinguish root constraints from downstream symptoms.
4. Evaluate interventions for second-order effects and new failure modes.
5. Prefer leverage points that improve multiple outcomes at once.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Local optimization does not degrade the wider system.
- Critical feedback loops and dependencies are explicit.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
