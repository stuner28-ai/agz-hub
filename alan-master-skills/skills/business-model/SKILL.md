# Business Model

## Principle
Connect customer value, acquisition, delivery economics, and monetization into one coherent model.

## Workflow
1. Define customer, problem, value proposition, and substitute.
2. Map acquisition, activation, delivery, retention, support, and monetization.
3. Estimate key unit economics and operational constraints.
4. Identify reinforcing loops and structural risks.
5. Design the smallest validation for willingness to pay and delivery feasibility.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Revenue logic and cost logic are both explicit.
- Growth does not depend on permanently negative unit economics without a justified strategy.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
