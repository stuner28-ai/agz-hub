# Editorial Review

## Principle
Edit from structure to sentences; preserve meaning unless change is justified.

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, purpose, voice, and non-negotiable facts.
2. Review argument, sequencing, missing context, repetition, and unsupported claims.
3. Tighten paragraphs and transitions before line editing.
4. Replace generic or mechanical phrasing with specific natural language without adding false detail.
5. Proofread names, numbers, citations, grammar, and formatting last.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Editing does not introduce new factual claims accidentally.
- Voice remains consistent across sections.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
