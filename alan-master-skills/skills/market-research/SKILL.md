# Market Research

## Principle
Build a decision-oriented view of a market from triangulated evidence.

## Workflow
1. Define the market boundary and customer problem.
2. Segment customers by behavior and need rather than demographics alone.
3. Triangulate demand using market data, search behavior, competitor traction, reviews, and direct research where available.
4. Estimate TAM/SAM/SOM with explicit assumptions when sizing is requested.
5. Conclude with demand evidence, risks, entry wedge, and validation experiments.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Sizing assumptions are visible and non-overlapping.
- Market enthusiasm is separated from demonstrated willingness to pay.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
