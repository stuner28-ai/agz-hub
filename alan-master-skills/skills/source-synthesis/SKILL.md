# Source Synthesis

## Principle
Synthesize across sources while preserving what each source actually supports.

## Workflow
1. Extract key claims, data, assumptions, and scope from each source.
2. Normalize terminology and identify direct agreements and contradictions.
3. Weight evidence by authority, directness, methodology, and recency.
4. Build a synthesis around themes or questions, not source-by-source summaries.
5. Mark unresolved disagreements and what additional evidence would resolve them.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- No source is made to support a claim it does not contain.
- Contradictions remain visible.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
