# Decision Analysis

## Principle
Make decisions by explicit criteria rather than preference drift.

## Workflow
1. Define alternatives and decision criteria.
2. Weight criteria only when relative importance is known; otherwise show an unweighted comparison.
3. Separate facts, estimates, and subjective judgments.
4. Test the leading option against failure scenarios and reversibility.
5. Recommend one option with the conditions that would change the recommendation.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- No false precision in weights or scores.
- Recommendation traces directly to criteria.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
