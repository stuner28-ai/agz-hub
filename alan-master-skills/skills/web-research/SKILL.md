# Web Research

## Principle
Retrieve the most relevant current evidence with minimal search noise.

## Workflow
1. Translate the request into precise search queries and date constraints.
2. Prefer official or first-party sources for specifications and policies; use strong journalism for events.
3. Open promising results and verify context rather than relying on snippets.
4. Cross-check material claims when sources could be stale, biased, or derivative.
5. Cite the evidence that directly supports the answer.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Search recency matches the claim.
- No unsupported current-state assertion.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
