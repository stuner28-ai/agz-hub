# Conversion Optimization

## Principle
Remove friction and uncertainty at the point where intent should become action.

## Workflow
1. Map funnel steps and quantify where users drop.
2. Segment by traffic source, device, intent, and new/returning users when useful.
3. Diagnose motivation, clarity, trust, friction, distraction, and technical failure.
4. Prioritize hypotheses by impact, confidence, and effort.
5. Run controlled experiments or staged changes with guardrail metrics.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Do not optimize a downstream step while upstream traffic quality is unknown.
- A lift is checked for revenue/quality side effects.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
