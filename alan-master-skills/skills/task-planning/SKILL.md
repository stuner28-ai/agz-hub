# Task Planning

## Principle
Turn a complex goal into an executable plan with checkpoints and evidence.

## Workflow
1. Define the desired end state and non-goals.
2. Identify dependencies, risks, and irreversible actions.
3. Break work into independently verifiable steps.
4. Attach an acceptance check to every major step.
5. Execute in dependency order and revise only when evidence requires it.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Every step produces a concrete output or decision.
- The plan is short enough to guide execution rather than replace it.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
