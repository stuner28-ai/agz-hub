# Product Discovery

## Principle
Reduce product risk by validating problem, user, value, and constraints before solution commitment.

## Workflow
1. Define target user, job, pain, current workaround, and desired outcome.
2. Separate evidence from assumptions.
3. Rank assumptions by impact and uncertainty.
4. Choose the cheapest research or experiment that can invalidate the riskiest assumption.
5. Translate validated learning into scope, non-goals, and next decision.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Discovery can produce a no-build decision.
- Evidence refers to behavior, not compliments alone.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
