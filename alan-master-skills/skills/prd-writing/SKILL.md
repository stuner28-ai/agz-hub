# Prd Writing

## Principle
Specify outcomes and observable behavior without over-prescribing implementation.

## Workflow
1. State problem, target user, objective, non-goals, and success metrics.
2. Define primary flows, requirements, edge cases, permissions, states, and dependencies.
3. Write measurable acceptance criteria.
4. Capture analytics, rollout, migration, support, and risk requirements as relevant.
5. List open decisions separately from committed requirements.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Requirements are testable.
- Solution details are included only when they are true constraints.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
