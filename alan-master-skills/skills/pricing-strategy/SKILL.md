# Pricing Strategy

## Principle
Price around customer value, segmentation, economics, and strategic position.

## Workflow
1. Define customer segments, use cases, alternatives, and value metric.
2. Estimate willingness-to-pay evidence and cost-to-serve.
3. Choose packaging and fences that align price with value received.
4. Model conversion, expansion, churn, margin, and cannibalization effects.
5. Design a controlled pricing test and migration policy.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- Discounts have a strategic purpose and expiration logic.
- Price is not derived from cost-plus alone unless appropriate for the market.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
