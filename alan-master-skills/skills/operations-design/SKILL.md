# Operations Design

## Principle
Create repeatable flow with clear ownership, capacity, quality, and exception handling.

## Workflow
1. Map demand, inputs, process steps, queues, handoffs, outputs, and owners.
2. Measure cycle time, work in progress, error/rework, capacity, and service level.
3. Remove bottlenecks and unnecessary handoffs before automating.
4. Define standard work, exception paths, controls, and escalation.
5. Instrument the process and review performance regularly.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- The process has one owner per decision.
- Automation follows process simplification, not chaos.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
