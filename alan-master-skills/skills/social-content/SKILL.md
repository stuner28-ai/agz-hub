# Social Content

## Principle
Earn attention quickly, deliver one clear value unit, and give the audience a reason to continue.

## Workflow
1. Define platform, audience, objective, content pillar, and format.
2. Write a strong opening that creates relevance without deceptive bait.
3. Deliver one coherent idea with concrete detail, proof, story, or demonstration.
4. Adapt pacing and length to the platform.
5. Use a call to action only when it advances the objective.

## Output contract
- State the result or decision clearly.
- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and unresolved uncertainty when material.
- Include only the detail required to make the output usable.

## Quality gates
- The hook is fulfilled by the content.
- Posts vary in mechanism instead of repeating one template.

## Stop conditions
- Stop when the requested outcome and quality gates are satisfied.
- Escalate rather than inventing missing authority, credentials, data, or irreversible permission.
